Insurance, Costs & Uninsured Options

Most Miami providers accept Delta Dental of Florida, Cigna, Aetna, UnitedHealthcare, MetLife, and common PPO plans. Emergency treatment without insurance is typically far cheaper than a hospital ER visit — and an ER cannot treat the underlying dental problem.

No insurance? Most clinics offer flexible financing, in-house payment plans, or sliding-scale fees for uninsured patients. Benefits verification is done by each clinic's own billing team — ask when you call.
