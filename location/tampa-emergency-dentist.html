Insurance, Costs & Uninsured Options

Most Tampa providers accept Delta Dental of Florida, Cigna, Aetna, UnitedHealthcare, MetLife, and common PPO plans. Emergency treatment without insurance is typically far cheaper than a Tampa General or St. Joseph's Hospital ER visit — and the ER cannot fix the underlying tooth.

No insurance? Most clinics offer flexible financing, in-house payment plans, or sliding-scale fees. Benefits verification is handled by each clinic's own billing team when you call to book.
